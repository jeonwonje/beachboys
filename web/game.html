ASI-301 BeachBoy

Wet Band

Collect the debris before the tide takes it. Your target is 80%, the rate that the real machine had to reach.

  • Cross-slope. Debris slides off the belt if you lean more than 5°. Face straight up or down the beach until it reaches the drum.
  • Jams. Long pieces wedge if you take them fast, and speed on wet sand builds sand at the skid. Reverse to clear a jam.
  • Clear-out. The bin holds 16 pieces. Stop in a yellow ring to empty it.
